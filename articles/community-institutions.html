Community institutions

The public desks people actually call in winter: the library, the Senior Center, the Chamber, Town Hall, Police, Fire Rescue, and Recreation — each with a verified phone number.

January 2027 candidate page · For emergencies, always call 911

Open the flipbook Open the pull-out map Article pool

These are not advertisements. They are the civic numbers and addresses we checked against official town, library, and Chamber pages. Hours change; phones and street addresses are the stable part. Confirm hours on each organization’s own site before you go.

In an emergency, call 911. The non-emergency lines below are for everything else.

Falmouth Public Library

Find it Downtown side C2 (also drawn on the Woods Hole side at D1).

Main Library: 300 Main Street, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 457-2555

East Falmouth Branch: 310 East Falmouth Highway, East Falmouth, MA 02536, (508) 548-6340

North Falmouth Branch: 6 Chester Street, North Falmouth, MA 02556, (508) 563-2922

Hours, event calendars, and branch closures stay on the library’s own site: Locations & Hours and the event calendar.

Falmouth Senior Center

Run by the Town’s Senior Services Department (Council on Aging), for residents 60 and older. 780 Main Street, Falmouth, MA 02540 — on Main Street between the Police Department (Downtown side E1) and the Gus Canty Community Center (Downtown side F1).

(508) 540-0196

The official town page lists building hours Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Programs appear in the Town’s Senior Happenings newsletter; most ask you to pre-register by phone. Official page: falmouthma.gov/444/Senior-Services-Senior-Center.

Falmouth Chamber of Commerce

Find it Downtown side C2, on Academy Lane near the library.

20 Academy Lane, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 548-8500

The official Chamber contact page lists hours Monday through Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Official page: Contact the Falmouth Chamber.

Falmouth Town Hall

Find it Downtown side B2 (also drawn on the Woods Hole side at D2).

59 Town Hall Square, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 548-7611

The official staff directory lists hours Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., except holidays. Department numbers stay on falmouthma.gov.

Falmouth Police Department

Find it Downtown side E1.

750 Main Street, Falmouth, MA 02540

Emergency: 911
Non-emergency: (774) 255-4527

Official page: falmouthma.gov/382/Police.

Falmouth Fire Rescue

Find it Downtown side C2, on Main Street near the library.

399 Main Street, Falmouth, MA 02540

Emergency: 911
Non-emergency: (508) 495-2500

Official page: falmouthma.gov/188/Fire-Rescue.

Falmouth Recreation · Gus Canty Community Center

Find it Downtown side F1.

790 Main Street, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 457-2567

Email: rec.dept@falmouthma.gov. The official Recreation page is the place for program sign-ups and current building hours. Fall hours were posted when we checked; confirm the January hours before you go. Official page: falmouthma.gov/384/Recreation.

Hours and programs: The phones and street addresses above were checked against official pages in November 2026. We do not copy weekly program lists here, because they go stale. Use each organization’s own site or phone for the hours you need on the day you visit.
