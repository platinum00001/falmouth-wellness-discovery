Local sports and schools

Winter with the Falmouth High Clippers, youth programs at town Recreation and the Y, and a short guide to the schools that shape the season.

January 2027 candidate page · No game dates are printed unless they come from an official schedule

Open the flipbook Open the pull-out map Article pool

When the January issue lands in waiting rooms, winter sports will already be underway. This page is a directory of official desks, not a scoreboard. Schedules move; the places to check them do not.

Falmouth High Clippers, winter 2026–27

Falmouth High School, the Clippers, is at 874 Gifford Street, Falmouth, MA 02540, (508) 540-2200. The athletic department’s official winter page listed registration for the 2026–27 winter season as open on FamilyID, with tryouts and practices for all winter sports beginning Monday, November 30, 2026. Athletes need a current physical on file.

The official winter coach list named these programs: boys basketball, girls basketball, boys ice hockey, girls ice hockey, track and field, gymnastics, and wrestling. A wrestling coach had not yet been named on that page when we checked it in mid-November 2026. Falmouth High competes under the MIAA in the Cape & Islands League. School colors, on the MIAA school profile, are maroon and white; the nickname is the Clippers.

We are not printing individual game dates here. The athletics page points families to ArbiterSports for current schedules, scores, and rosters, and to the MIAA school profile for results. Some Field House and turf events are carried on the NFHS Network; ice-arena games may also appear on LiveBarn. For questions, the listed contacts are Athletic Director Kristin Harkins, (508) 540-2200 ext. 3022, and athletic administrative assistant Christina Ball, ext. 3037.

Confirm: Home dates and start times belong on the official Falmouth High School Athletics page and ArbiterSports. If a date is not on those calendars, it does not belong in the magazine.

Youth sports: town Recreation and the Y

The Town of Falmouth Recreation Department is at the Gus Canty Community Center, 790 Main Street, Falmouth, MA 02540, (508) 457-2567, rec.dept@falmouthma.gov. The official page describes affordable programs for all ages and abilities, and it posts facility hours there. The fall hours on that page should not be treated as the January hours; confirm winter hours before you go. On the pull-out map, Gus Canty is Downtown side F1, on Main Street just beyond the Police Department at Downtown E1.

YMCA Cape Cod already serves Falmouth with early education, before- and after-school care at several Falmouth elementary schools, and summer day camps, including Camp Falmouth at Morse Pond School, 323 Jones Road, and a Woods Hole camp. The main phone is (508) 362-6500, ymcacapecod.org. A new Upper Cape branch, the Don & Dottie Smith YMCA, is being built at 487 Brick Kiln Road in Falmouth. A topping-out ceremony was held in September 2026; the building was not open when we checked, and an opening date for winter 2027 had not been posted. Do not treat Brick Kiln Road as a drop-in desk until the Y announces that it is open.

Confirm: Winter youth-league sign-ups and vacation-week programs change by season. Use the Recreation and YMCA pages, not this magazine, for the current session.

The schools

These are the four schools the January issue set out to include, with addresses and phones from the official Falmouth Public Schools directory and, for the independent school, from Falmouth Academy’s contact page. Grades are as those pages list them.

Falmouth High School

Grades 9–12 · 874 Gifford Street, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 540-2200

Principal: Dr. Alan Harris. The regular school day is 7:22 a.m. to 2:03 p.m. Official site: fhs.falmouth.k12.ma.us.

Lawrence School

Grades 7–8 · 113 Lakeview Avenue, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 548-0606

Official site: law.falmouth.k12.ma.us.

Morse Pond School

Grades 5–6 · 323 Jones Road, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 548-7300

YMCA Cape Cod’s Camp Falmouth uses this campus in the summer. Official site: mp.falmouth.k12.ma.us.

Falmouth Academy

Independent school, grades 7–12 · 7 Highfield Drive, Falmouth, MA 02540

(508) 457-9696

Next to Highfield Hall & Gardens (Downtown side A1 on the pull-out map). Official site: falmouthacademy.org.

The Falmouth Public Schools 2026–27 calendar, approved by the School Committee, lists no school on Monday, January 18, 2027 (Martin Luther King Jr. Day); winter recess Monday–Friday, February 15–19, 2027, with classes resuming Monday, February 22; and no school on Friday, March 26, 2027. Those are official school dates, not sports or event listings.
