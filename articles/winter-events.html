Candidate for print

Winter events, January–March 2027

What we can already confirm for the new year — and what still needs a check with the organizer before it goes into print.

January 2027 candidate page · Dates below are either verified from an official source or clearly marked “Confirm”

Open the flipbook Open the pull-out map Article pool

A winter calendar for a January magazine has to be honest. Some 2027 dates are already on official sites. Others follow last year’s pattern and are not yet posted, and those stay in a confirm box rather than looking like a promise.

Verified on official calendars

  • Fri Jan 1, 2027

    Verified New Year’s Day — Falmouth Public Schools closed

    The official 2026–27 Falmouth Public Schools calendar lists January 1 as a New Year’s Day observance, with no school. Classes resume Monday, January 4, 2027.

  • Sat Jan 2, 2027

    Verified Scavenger hunt at the East Falmouth Library

    9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at the East Falmouth Branch, 310 East Falmouth Highway. The official January 2027 library calendar also lists beginner Mah Jongg open play at the branch from 11:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. that day. Phone (508) 548-6340.

  • Mon Jan 4, 2027

    Verified Standing programs at Falmouth Public Library

    The official January 2027 calendar lists Maker Mondays at the East Falmouth Branch, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., as a drop-in science activity for children; Next Chapter Book Club at the Main Library, 11:15 a.m. to 12:45 p.m.; and beginner Mah Jongg open play at the Main Library, 1:00 to 3:00 p.m. The Main Library is at 300 Main Street, Downtown side C2, (508) 457-2555.

  • Wed Jan 6, 2027

    Verified Retro Game Extravaganza

    5:30 to 8:00 p.m. in the Young Adult Room at the Main Library, 300 Main Street. Listed on the official January 2027 library calendar.

  • Thu Jan 7, 2027

    Verified Digital Navigators technology help

    1:00 to 2:00 p.m. in the Lemcke Reference Room at the Main Library, with an FCTV Digital Navigator offering free one-on-one help with Libby, Hoopla, and Kanopy. The listing describes this as a weekly Thursday session; confirm later dates on the library calendar.

  • Mon Jan 18, 2027

    Verified Martin Luther King Jr. Day — schools closed

    The official Falmouth Public Schools calendar lists no school on Monday, January 18, 2027. Town desks may also be closed for the holiday; confirm hours with each office before you go.

  • Jan 22–Feb 6, 2027

    Verified Falmouth Theatre Guild, One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest

    The Guild’s official 2026–27 season page lists this production from January 22 through February 6, 2027, at 58 Highfield Drive, Falmouth, near Highfield Hall (Downtown side A1). Showtimes: Friday 7:30 p.m., Saturday 2:00 p.m., Sunday 2:00 p.m. Tickets and details: falmouththeatreguild.org, (508) 548-0400.

  • Feb 15–19, 2027

    Verified Falmouth Public Schools winter recess

    No school Monday through Friday, February 15–19, 2027. Classes resume Monday, February 22, 2027, per the official 2026–27 calendar. Vacation-week programs at Recreation or the Y should be confirmed with those desks; they were not posted as 2027 dates when we checked.

  • Fri Mar 26, 2027

    Verified No school

    The official Falmouth Public Schools calendar lists Friday, March 26, 2027, as a day with no school.

  • Sat–Sun Mar 20–21, 2027

    Verified Falmouth Chorale, Of the Sea

    March 20 and March 21, 2027, at 3:00 p.m., at Falmouth Academy, 7 Highfield Drive, next to Highfield Hall (Downtown side A1). The official chorale page lists works by Edward Elgar, Saburō Takata, and Eric Whitacre, with piano, strings, and theorbo. Details: falmouthchorale.org/of-the-sea, (774) 392-2383.

Still to confirm before print

Confirm Winter Farmers Market, 2027. In 2026 the Falmouth Farmers Market held a Sunday winter market at Mullen Hall Elementary School. The 2027 dates and location were not posted on falmouthfarmersmarket.com when we checked in November 2026. Do not print a 2027 address or time until the market publishes them.

Confirm Highfield Hall & Gardens winter events. The official Highfield calendar had no January–March 2027 events posted when we checked. Confirm at highfieldhallandgardens.org/calendar before adding any date.

Confirm Clipper home games. Winter sports tryouts and practices were set to begin Monday, November 30, 2026, but individual game dates are not printed here. Check Falmouth High School Athletics and ArbiterSports.

Confirm Library programs in February and March 2027, Senior Center calendars, and Chamber events. January library programs are already posted. Later months, Senior Happenings, and Chamber event dates should be taken from each organizer’s own calendar just before print, not from last year’s pattern.

For standing library programs after January 2, use the live calendar at falmouthpubliclibrary.org. For town programs, start with falmouthma.gov and the community institutions page.
