Falmouth MA beauty: tanning, hair, and nails

Tanorama is the featured tanning salon at 365 Main Street. Owner Scott has kept it on Main Street for nearly three decades. A phone, a website, and hours are not shown, because Tanorama has not confirmed them for this directory.

Bellezza Salon Medspa is at 221 Main Street, and Spa & Nails is at 669 Main Street. Both Main Street dots are approximate. Services and prices stay off this page until each salon confirms them.

Before you call

  • Call the salon for hours. We do not print a guessed closing time.
  • Ask what a first visit includes before you book.
  • The Tanorama story in the magazine is a sample layout until the interview with Scott is finished.
