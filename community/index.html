Falmouth MA · Community

Falmouth MA community: library, senior center, youth sports, Town Hall, and the Chamber

These are public desks, not paid ads. The magazine gives them a quarterly 'what's happening' space. This directory lists where they are and links to the sites they already run.

Hours and programs change, so check each organization's site before you go. A youth league can list its season here for free.

Two more walking-map pins sit with this page: Falmouth Elks Lodge #2380 at 140 Palmer Avenue, and Tommy's Place, listed as a charity, at 26 Elm Arch Way. Events and programs are not copied here.

Before you call

  • For an emergency, call 911. This page is not a dispatch line.
  • Library events belong on falmouthpubliclibrary.org. We will add a quarterly note only when the library sends one.
  • Town Hall business starts at falmouthma.gov. Bring the document the department asked for.

Falmouth Senior Center

The Falmouth Senior Center, run by the Town's Senior Services Department (Council on Aging), at 780 Main Street. Health services, referrals, fitness classes, education and recreation for residents 60 and older. Building hours Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4 p.m.
