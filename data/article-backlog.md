**Angle:** Go beyond the January mention of St. Anthony’s and “Strawberry Sunday.” Follow the East Falmouth and Davisville fields, how berries were shipped off Cape, and what remains of that landscape and community today — including the stained glass and memory at the church.

**Why spring:** Strawberry season and the farming calendar belong in a spring issue.

**Starting sources (already used in January, not yet a full feature):**

**Starting sources:**

**Still to do:** Confirm the aquarium reopening date before the April issue goes to press; ask WHOI and the MBL for visitor guidance; keep readers out of working labs.
