Falmouth MA dental: family, cosmetic, and implant dentistry

Dental care in Falmouth includes family and preventive visits, cosmetic work such as whitening, and implants.

The Featured slot on this page is open. Sliding-fee dental care at the Community Health Center is listed on our Free Food & Help page.

Before you call

  • Check that the office takes your dental insurance before you book.
  • Ask for a written estimate before cosmetic or implant work.
  • For a dental emergency after hours, call the office first. Its voicemail usually gives the on-call number.
