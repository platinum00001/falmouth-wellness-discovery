Falmouth MA · Doctors

Falmouth MA doctors, urgent care, and dental visits

This page is a directory, not medical advice, and it does not rank clinicians. For an emergency, call 911.

Routine visits, urgent care, and dental care each get one featured partner. Dental offices at Homeport are listed on the Dental page.

Before you call

  • Call 911 for an emergency. Do not use this page as a substitute for emergency care.
  • Ask a clinic what to bring: insurance card, medication list, and the reason for the visit.
  • Urgent care and the emergency department are not the same door. The practice can tell you which one fits.
