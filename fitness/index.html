Falmouth MA fitness: gyms, yoga, and personal training

A gym, a yoga class, and a session with a trainer are different promises. This page will feature one confirmed business in each, with a button that goes to that business.

rooted Hot Yoga is on the walking map at 80 Davis Straits, and Fitness Directions, a Pilates studio, is at 27 Falmouth Heights Road. Check each studio's website for class times. Featured gym and trainer spots are open to partners.

Before you call

  • Ask what a first visit includes and whether you can pay for one class before a membership.
  • Ask a trainer where sessions happen: their studio, a gym, or your home.
  • If you are returning from an injury, ask the studio what they need from you before you book. This page is not medical advice.
