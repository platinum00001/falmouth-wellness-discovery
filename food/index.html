Falmouth MA · Food

Falmouth MA food: breakfast, lunch, dinner, trucks, and farm stands

Falmouth eats on Main Street, in the villages, from trucks when the season is on, and at farm stands. This page keeps one card for each of those, and only prints what the business has confirmed.

The confirmed lunch partner is Cape Island Café, an alcohol-free, sober-friendly weekday lunch café at 316 Gifford Street. Breakfast, dinner, a food truck, and a farm stand are open sample slots.

Before you call

  • Weekday lunch at Cape Island is alcohol-free and sober-friendly. The menu is the café's to publish, not ours to invent.
  • Food trucks and farm stands change with the season. A card without a schedule means we do not have a confirmed one.
  • Call ahead if you need to know about ingredients. We do not post allergy claims for a kitchen we have not heard from.
