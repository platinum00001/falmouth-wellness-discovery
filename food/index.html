Falmouth MA · Food

Falmouth MA food: breakfast, lunch, dinner, cafés, and bakeries

Falmouth eats on Main Street, around the harbor, in Woods Hole and out in the villages. This page lists local restaurants, cafés and bakeries with their own phone numbers and websites.

The featured lunch partner is Cape Island Café, an alcohol-free, sober-friendly weekday lunch café at 316 Gifford Street. A food truck is still an open sample slot. Hours shown are as published by each restaurant and change by season, so call ahead.

Before you call

  • Weekday lunch at Cape Island is alcohol-free and sober-friendly. The menu is the café's to publish, not ours to invent.
  • Food trucks and farm stands change with the season. A card without a schedule means we do not have a confirmed one.
  • Call ahead if you need to know about ingredients. We do not post allergy claims for a kitchen we have not heard from.
