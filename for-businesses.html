Who we are

The publisher of Falmouth Wellness Discovery

Health & Wellness Maps LLC publishes the free quarterly Falmouth Wellness Discovery magazine and the fold-out walking map that comes with it. Each issue is organized around Mind, Body, Soul, Health & Food.

The magazine is free for readers. A thousand copies go out each quarter into waiting rooms, hotel lobbies, the Chamber, and the library. The map is the 11×17 fold-out people keep and walk with, from Main Street and Homeport out into town.

That is the heart of wellness business marketing in Falmouth MA: a printed guide neighbors and visitors already pick up, published by a company based in Falmouth.
