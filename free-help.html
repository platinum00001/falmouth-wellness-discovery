Free meals, food pantries, recovery meetings, hotlines and other free services in Falmouth, Woods Hole, East, North and West Falmouth, Teaticket, Waquoit and Hatchville, plus nearby Mashpee, Bourne and Sandwich. A few Hyannis and regional services are included where nothing like them runs on the Upper Cape.
