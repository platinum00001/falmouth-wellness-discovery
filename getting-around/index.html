Falmouth MA getting around: taxi, bike rentals, and the Shining Sea Bikeway

Getting to your wellness stops is half the plan. Falmouth has a 24-hour local taxi and bike shops that rent bikes for the Shining Sea Bikeway, which runs between North Falmouth and Woods Hole.

Corner Cycle is listed on the Bike Shop page. The Transportation page holds our featured local guide. The Featured slot on this page is open.

Before you call

  • Book airport and ferry rides ahead in summer.
  • Bike rental shops often need ID and a deposit. Ask about helmets and child seats when you call.
  • Bus routes and the ferry change by season. Check CCRTA and the Steamship Authority before you travel.
