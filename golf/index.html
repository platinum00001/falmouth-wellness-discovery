Falmouth MA golf

Golf around Falmouth depends on the season, the tee sheet, and whether a course is public that week. This page will feature one confirmed course or club and link out for tee times.

A local course or club can take the featured spot, with its address, tee times and booking link.

Before you call

  • Ask if the tee time is walking or cart, and what the weather policy is.
  • Public and private play are different invitations. The course should say which one the listing is.
  • Winter hours are the course's to publish. We will not copy a summer schedule into a winter magazine.
