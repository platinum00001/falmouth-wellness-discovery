Falmouth Visitor Guide

Two grids help you find things. The pull-out map in the middle of the magazine (the illustrated Falmouth Health & Wellness map) uses letters A–F across and numbers 1–4 down, on both sides: the front is the Downtown side, the back is the Woods Hole side. The interactive map below is the online town grid, columns A–H from west to east and rows 1–6 from north to south, from Palmer Avenue to Teaticket Highway. A date says check date unless the published date is printed here.

Find it on the pull-out map

The illustrated pull-out in the centre of the magazine has its own grid: letters A–F across the top and bottom, numbers 1–4 down the sides, inside the partner border. The front is the Downtown side (Downtown · Main Street); the back is the Woods Hole side (Main Street & Woods Hole). Some places, such as the Village Green and the library, are drawn on both sides. Beaches marked “inset” are in the Beaches up north box, not to scale with the main map.
