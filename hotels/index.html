Falmouth MA hotels: where to stay

Palmer House Inn is on the walking map at 81 Palmer Avenue. Rates, amenities, and a booking link are not listed until the inn confirms them.

Other hotels can still take an open card. Nothing else is named here.

Before you call

  • Ask whether the rate is for the room you will actually sleep in, and what is closed in winter.
  • If you want to walk to Main Street, ask how far the door is. We will not guess a walk time.
  • Book on the hotel's own link when we have one, so the hotel can see the visit.
