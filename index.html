What it is

Falmouth Wellness Discovery is a local quarterly: stories people pick up and keep, plus a map that shows where to go next. Copies are free to readers.

1,000 free copies each quarter
4× a year: Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall
Free in the rooms where people wait
11×17 fold-out discovery map inside

Copies are placed by hand in waiting rooms, hotel lobbies, the Chamber, the library, and other local stops that agree to host them. We do not mail the magazine into a pile of bills.

Four pillars

Every issue is health, food, fitness, mind, and soul. General town news lives somewhere else.

Mind

Innovators, learning, and the people trying something new.

Body

Fitness, yoga, movement, and the sports kids are actually playing.

Soul

Family stories, good deeds, a charity spotlight, and pets.

Health & Food

Breakfast through dinner, farm stands, and the pharmacy counter.

Winter 2027 is a sample you can turn page by page

The digital issue has the cover, a letter, Tanorama on Main Street, founding partners Cape Island Café and Falmouth Transport, and a pull-out you can tear out: a wellness map on the front and a Falmouth visitor guide on the back. Open cards, a crossword, a sample calendar, and the audio-update code are still in the issue.

Real picks replace the “your business here” cards later. The copy lives in one file, data/issue1.json, so a confirmed story can be dropped in without rebuilding the site.
