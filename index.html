What it is

A local quarterly people keep

Stories you pick up in a waiting room and take home, plus a map that shows where to go next. Free to readers, paid for by local partners.

1,000 free copies each quarter
4× a year: Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall
Free in the rooms where people wait
11×17 fold-out walking map inside

Every issue

Four pillars

Health, food, fitness, mind and soul. General town news lives somewhere else.

Mind

Innovators, learning, a good bookshop and the people trying something new.

Body

Yoga, pilates, recovery, healthy aging and the sports kids are actually playing.

Soul

Family stories, good deeds, a charity spotlight and adoptable pets.

Health & Food

Breakfast through dinner, farm stands, nutrition and the pharmacy counter.

For local businesses

Partners keep it printing

The core Featured package is $500 a month, billed monthly, one business per category. Founding partners take 25% off. You get the featured card on your category page, a numbered pin on the walking map and a place in the magazine. Prices are introductory and subject to change.
