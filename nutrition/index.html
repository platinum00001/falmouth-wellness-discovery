Falmouth MA nutrition: health food, smoothie bars, and supplement shops

This page is for the everyday side of eating well in Falmouth: a vitamin and health food store, a juice and smoothie bar, and lunch spots that cook with fresh ingredients.

The Falmouth Farmers Market runs Thursdays at Marine Park in season. The Featured slot on this page is open; listings below are directory entries, not paid placements.

Before you call

  • Supplements are not medicine. Ask your doctor or pharmacist before you start one, especially with a prescription.
  • Menus and hours change with the season. Call or check the business's own site before you go.
  • Allergy questions belong with the kitchen. This directory does not make allergy or health claims for any business.
