How billing works

Partnerships are a monthly subscription, invoiced and charged monthly, even though the magazine prints quarterly. Payment is monthly auto-pay by a card on file through Square, using a secure Square checkout or invoice link.

You can cancel anytime after an initial 3-month founding term. That 3-month term is proposed. It is not in force until you agree to it on the Square invoice.

This website never asks for a card number. The button below each package is a placeholder until a Square link is pasted into config.js.

Gift cards and the monthly bill

You may pay up to 50% of a month’s bill in your own gift cards. The first 25% of the bill paid in gift cards is credited at full face value. Any gift-card amount between 25% and 50% is credited at 75% of face value.

Example on the $500 Featured package (list price, before the founding discount): $125 in gift cards = $125 credit. $250 in gift cards = $218.75 credit, so cash due is $281.25.
