The problem

Local ads too often land where nobody is looking

Lost in the mail pile

Home-mailed magazines compete with bills, flyers and catalogs, and many get recycled unopened.

Priced for big budgets

A national-chain magazine package was quoted locally at about $1,000 a month. That’s out of reach for most Main Street shops.

Online listings left to drift

Wrong hours, old prices and missing photos on Google, with no one local to call for help fixing them.

Meanwhile, Falmouth spends real time waiting: at the doctor, the dentist, the courthouse, the salon and the ferry.

~$1,000/mo is a typical community magazine package quoted locally. The other points are positioning, not statistics.

Flexible payment

Ways to pay that work for small businesses

50% max

Pay part in gift cards

Cover up to half of any monthly bill with your own gift cards. The first 25% of the bill is credited at full face value. Any amount between 25% and 50% is credited at 75% of face value. We use the cards for reader contests and host thank-yous.

4 ads

Nonprofit sponsorship

For $400, a nonprofit can sponsor four eighth-page ads for small businesses in our Small Business Spotlight.

Free

Host locations

Every waiting room that hosts copies gets a free eighth-page listing and a pin on the Discovery Map.
