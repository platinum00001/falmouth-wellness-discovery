Falmouth MA · Main Street Shops

Falmouth Main Street and Homeport shops: jewelry, boutiques, kids and home

Falmouth's Main Street runs from the Village Green past Town Hall Square and the library toward Davis Straits. Most of the shops here are owner-run, and many have been on the street for years.

Homeport is the cluster of offices and shops on Gifford Street at Jones Road, home to Cape Island Café, a dental group, a chiropractor, a nutrition shop and a family insurance agency.

Before you call

  • Main Street parking is metered in season; the lots behind the library and off Town Hall Square are a short walk.
  • Hours change between summer and winter, so call ahead in the off-season.
  • Homeport has its own parking lot off Gifford Street.
