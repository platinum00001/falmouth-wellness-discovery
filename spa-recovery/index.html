Falmouth MA · Spa & Recovery

Falmouth MA spa and recovery: massage, physical therapy, chiropractic, and day spas

Recovery means different things: a massage after a long week, physical therapy after an injury, a chiropractic visit, or a peer community for people in recovery from substance use.

These are directory listings with their own phone numbers and websites. The Featured slot on this page is open. Nothing here is medical advice.

Before you call

  • Physical therapy and chiropractic may need a referral or insurance approval. Ask your insurer before you book.
  • Massage therapists in Massachusetts are licensed. You can ask to see a license number.
  • If you or someone near you is in crisis, call or text 988, or dial 911. Free help is also listed on our Free Food & Help page.
