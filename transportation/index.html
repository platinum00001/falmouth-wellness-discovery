Falmouth MA · Transportation

Falmouth MA transportation: rides, taxis, and moving things around town

Falmouth runs from the village and Main Street out to Woods Hole, East Falmouth, North Falmouth, and the beaches. People need a ride to the ferry, a taxi when the car is in the shop, or help moving something across town.

This page lists local transportation partners and sends you to them. Falmouth Transport is the featured guide. Taxi companies can take the open cards. Nothing is filled in until that company confirms the phone, the fare, and the booking link.

Before you call

  • Ask whether the ride is a taxi, a scheduled car, or help moving a piece of furniture. Those are different jobs.
  • For the Woods Hole ferry, leave time for the terminal. This page does not publish a ferry schedule.
  • If a card has no phone, the business has not given us one yet. Please do not guess a number from an old listing.
